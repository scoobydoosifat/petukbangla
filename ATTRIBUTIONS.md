# Data and image attributions

- Traditional-food associations were curated from the public district guide at [Unseen Bangladesh](https://unseenbangladesh.com/). Entries that were crafts or other non-food products were excluded. Narayanganj and Jamalpur are intentionally not assigned a food because the reference data does not provide one.
- Food thumbnails are local copies of the reference guide's credited images. Their original authors and licenses remain listed by the reference guide; most originate from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons licenses. The application retains a source-credit field on every food record.
- Bangladesh district boundaries are from the Bangladesh Directorate General of Health Services public `All_Districts` ArcGIS feature layer. Geometry is simplified and coordinate-rounded for browser performance.
- Dhaka exploration tiles are © OpenStreetMap contributors and are attributed in the map UI.
- The landing-page food photograph is loaded from Unsplash.

Before a public commercial launch, replace any generic substitute thumbnail with a verified food-specific image and preserve its individual author/license metadata.
